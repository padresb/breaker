# PRD: Triple-A Fun Visuals and Sounds

## Goal
Make the game feel more cinematic and satisfying with richer visual effects and stronger audio feedback.

## User story
As a player, I want the game to look and sound more explosive and polished so that every action feels satisfying.

## Scope
- Add more distinct audio cues for gameplay events.
- Increase visual juice during hits, wave transitions, overdrive, and finale states.
- Add subtle ambient motion/lighting to the arena and HUD.
- Preserve the current controls and core gameplay loop.

## Non-goals
- No asset pipeline, no external audio files, no dependencies.
- No gameplay rebalance unless needed to support the new presentation.
- No major UI redesign.

## Acceptance criteria
- Bricks, powerups, wave transitions, and special states feel more distinct audiovisually.
- The game still loads and runs in-browser.
- Existing controls and progression continue to work.
- Verification passes after implementation.
